# HUD navigation and battery

Press Ctrl + backtick or choose Open overlay from the menu bar. Use the side buttons to switch sections, scroll the right-side list for more, and click the lower-left card for your profile. Storage and Activity Monitor sit below the center.

Escape backs out of editing or a menu before closing the HUD. Closing keeps EndfieldHUD running. The red power button asks before playing the closing animation and quitting.

The battery bar expands on hover and otherwise becomes a circle after its initial display. Charge/battery notifications use the selected language. CHARGE MODE describes the visual charging state; it does not promise a higher charging speed. Device readings depend on what macOS and the accessory expose.

Display settings control appearance, tilt, animations and which screen hosts the HUD. Work Mode can continue while the HUD is hidden.
