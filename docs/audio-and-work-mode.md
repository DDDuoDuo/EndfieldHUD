# Volume and Work Mode

## Volume

Choose an output or input device, then adjust volume, mute and stereo balance where the device supports them. Some external outputs have fixed volume and must be adjusted on the device itself.

Experimental per-app volume needs macOS 14.2 or newer and System Audio Recording permission. Lowering a supported app's slider starts its audio route; returning it to 100% keeps that route running at full volume. See [per-app audio](per-app-audio.md) for the supported devices.

## Work Mode

Choose Countdown or Stopwatch. Countdown has 5-, 30- and 60-minute presets and a custom duration. Start, Pause, Resume and Reset control the session. Timers continue while the HUD is hidden, and completed work time appears on your personal card.

Automatic macOS Focus uses Accessibility permission and recognizable Control Center controls. The timer works without Focus automation. An existing Focus is preserved. If the controls are unavailable, configured macOS Shortcuts can provide a fallback on macOS 13 or newer.
