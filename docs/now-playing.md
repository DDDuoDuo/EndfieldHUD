# Now Playing

Playback connects automatically to supported music apps. The HUD shows the title, artist, square album art and available timed lyrics. Use Previous, Play/Pause and Next, seek along the progress bar, or open the vertical volume control. The lyrics button hides or shows lyrics.

Lyrics show the current line with the surrounding lines faded. If no timed lyrics are supplied by the player, LRCLIB can provide them when a matching track is available. Some players expose less information or fewer controls, and macOS may request Automation permission.

With no active track, the section shows No music playing. Covers and lyrics depend on the player and available metadata.
