# Account linking

Choose Mainland China or Global, then Connect to open the official community login. After signing in, choose your game role and the profile fields you want to sync. While Sync personal profile is enabled, your game name, # number, UID and awakening date are read-only. Turn sync off to edit them.

Endfield can supply sanity, player ID, # number, awakening date, levels, collection counts and an avatar when available. Arknights supports sanity. Mainland China has been tested with both games; Global still needs live testing.

Click the sanity bar to see its recovery details and refresh controls. Recovery is calculated locally between requests: one point every 7 minutes 12 seconds for Endfield, or every 6 minutes for Arknights. Automatic refresh runs when the account panel or sanity display is visible and the last sample is at least ten minutes old. Manual refresh has a short repeat guard. Spending sanity in-game appears after the next refresh.

Linking is optional. Scoped community credentials stay in macOS Keychain; Disconnect removes the selected region's saved connection. A failed refresh keeps the last displayed data.
