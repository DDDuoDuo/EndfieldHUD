# Media Assembly

Open or drop a picture or video, or choose one from the Temporary File Shelf. Zoom and pan the preview while editing. The × below Open closes the current source.

Crop uses corner handles. Rotate, mirror, adjustments, curves and levels sit in the scrollable editing drawer. Pictures support Endfield filters and stickers; move, resize or rotate stickers directly with their handles. Video trim uses one range slider with draggable start and end points, and the preview seeks to the handle being moved.

Export to a new location or explicitly overwrite the source. Edits do not change the original until export succeeds. Supported formats and codecs depend on the media and macOS.
