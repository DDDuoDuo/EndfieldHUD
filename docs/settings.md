# Settings

## System

Choose the HUD language and startup behavior. Languages include English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean. System follows your Mac's language.

## Display

Adjust the theme, accent color, size, position, blur, brightness and pointer tilt. Choose a display for the HUD, a clock style and 12- or 24-hour time. Customize the app/menu bar icon and center logo. Ambient motion, Reduce Motion and low-power visual options control animation.

## Hotkeys

Record a new shortcut to summon the HUD. Escape cancels capture. Choose a combination that does not conflict with your other apps.

## About

Check the installed version, credits and updates. Automatic update controls are here; manual Check for Updates is also available in the menu bar.
