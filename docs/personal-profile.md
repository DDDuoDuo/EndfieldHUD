# Personal ID

Click the lower-left card to edit your profile. The three dots beneath the portrait open name, portrait and reset controls. Adjust portrait zoom and position, including zoom up to 20×.

Choose or restore a card background, then adjust its width and position. The card's own theme color applies to this section and the lower-left card. The eye button hides the text to show the background.

Names allow 20 characters and the number after # allows 10. Permission level is 1–60; exploration level is 1–7. Collection counts accept nonnegative numbers. Your introduction allows 150 characters.

Click the date box to switch between awakening date and birthday, then edit the date. Work Mode hours appear in the construction overview. Account linking can sync selected game fields while keeping manual editing available.
