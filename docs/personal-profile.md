# Personal Profile

**Personal Profile / 个人名片** opens from the lower-left identity card in the retained HUD. The center uses the supplied reference's portrait, awakening date, level rows, counters and work-hours layout; the right side contains an editable introduction with reference-style quotation marks and a pencil control. The shell, perspective and section transitions stay active.

The initial name is **Endministrator#0000**. The three-dot menu is drawn inside the HUD and edits the name/tag, chooses a portrait, or restores the default portrait. Names are limited to 20 characters and tags to 10; extra characters are silently truncated. The introduction accepts up to 150 characters. The background toolbar chooses or restores an image, changes its width and position, and adjusts the compact lower-left card’s crop independently. New and restored backgrounds default to **600 design points** wide; the editable range remains **400–900**, and saved custom widths are preserved. The background can extend beyond the circular viewport with soft edges. The eye control hides the profile text while retaining the background and the control to show the text again. Imported images are owned locally so the originals need not remain in place. Without a portrait, the existing silhouette remains. Profile changes also update the lower-left identity card.

Click the permission level to edit **1–60** (default **60**), or exploration level for **1–7** (default **7**). The three counters start at **24**, **54**, **325** and accept nonnegative whole numbers. Return saves an inline field; Escape discards its edit. Levels clamp to the nearest limit; nonnumeric input uses the default level. Image loading or storage failures can still display an error.

Portrait zoom (**1×–20×**) and horizontal/vertical crop offsets are saved independently of the background and its thumbnail crop. Older cards start with a centered portrait at **1×**. Choosing a new portrait or restoring the default resets those portrait adjustments; changing or restoring the background preserves them.

The portrait and background adjustment menus use themed sliders for zoom, width, and horizontal/vertical position. Dragging previews the crop and geometry immediately in the full profile and compact card; releasing saves the result. Closing a menu or leaving the profile also saves an in-progress adjustment. Portrait and compact-background crop offsets range from −100% to 100%; full-background movement ranges from −400 to 400 horizontally and −250 to 250 vertically. Keyboard arrows adjust the selected slider, and the same bounded values are available through native slider accessibility. Opening an adjustment menu preserves parallax; only an active drag holds the surface steady.

New portraits preserve their original encoded source pixels and orientation rather than reducing the source to 512 pixels. The shared renderer orients and crops that lazy image before producing a small display-resolution bitmap for each card; the full image is not uploaded as a layer texture. Imports are limited to 128 MB, 128 megapixels, and 32,768 pixels on either side. Backgrounds retain their existing 2048-pixel cap. Older managed 512-pixel portraits remain readable but cannot recover discarded detail; reselect the original image to restore it. Internal source restoration can preserve an existing zoom and position while replacing that reduced asset.

**苏醒日 / Awakening Day** initially records first use for new installations and can be edited. Existing installations migrate the earliest available local app-data creation date, since macOS does not expose a reliable historic installation date. The date label can switch to **生日 / Birthday**, which stores and displays only a month and day, including February 29. The selected mode and both date values persist independently; switching modes never discards either date. Birthday starts from the saved awakening month/day until edited. The numeric UID is randomly generated once and retained locally; editing either date does not change it. Neither is an account identifier or an online service.

The region overview reports cumulative Work Mode hours. Running countdown and stopwatch time counts; paused time does not. Resetting a timer or changing modes preserves the cumulative total. Tracking uses the existing monotonic clock and checkpoints at lifecycle/state changes, without another background sampling loop. Earlier Work Mode sessions before this feature are not reconstructed from the bounded event log.

Profile data is separate from visual preferences, so restoring Settings defaults preserves the profile. Diagnostic UI/test launches use temporary profile storage.

The background adjustment popup is anchored immediately above **更换名片主题 / Card theme**. Its color swatch opens five presets and a native custom color wheel. Card color initially follows the HUD theme, and **跟随浮层主题 / Follow HUD theme** restores that relationship. A custom card color affects only the full personal card and its compact lower-left counterpart, including their portrait frames and interaction highlights; it does not recolor the other HUD modules.

The compact card uses a rounded silhouette without an offset backing shadow. Its tag sits immediately beneath the name; the Authority badge uses the folded-plate icon with a small triangle, displays **MAX / 满级** only at level 60, and fills its progress bar by level/60. The Authority and maximum-level labels share a right edge. Their backing fades toward the left, keeping most of its dark weight beneath the icon on the right. The bar, maximum-level label and both card surfaces share the personal-card accent.
