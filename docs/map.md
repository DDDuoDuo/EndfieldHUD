# Map

Map shares the retained circular HUD, perspective, theme and section transitions. It combines offline NOAA ETOPO 2022 relief with raised Natural Earth country plates, shaded sides, bright outlines and diagonal hatching. The country beneath the center uses the HUD theme color. Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao are grouped into the China plate for this application's requested display. See [terrain provenance and preparation](map-data.md) for sources, presentation grouping, licenses and resolution.

- Drag with the left mouse button to pan. Longitude wraps across the date line; latitude stops at the poles.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom around the pointer. The `+` and `−` controls zoom around the center, from 2.1× to 128×. The minimum magnification and latitude bounds keep rectangular map edges outside the circular viewport, even near the poles.
- The initial view centers on Shenzhen at 3×. The crosshair resets zoom to 3× while retaining the current geographic center, subject to the polar bounds. Schema 4 upgrades the original version-1 overview and unchanged version-2/3 default cameras once; it preserves custom version-2/3 views and every pin. Subsequent launches retain the user's saved view. Visible place labels and gesture instructions are omitted; accessibility help retains the gesture instructions.
- Right-click terrain to place a pin. The ring control on the right also places one at the map center.
- Click a pin to select it and see its coordinates. The small `×` or Delete removes that selected pin. Right-clicking an existing pin selects it instead of stacking another at the same point. Escape clears selection before closing the HUD.
- Arrow keys move the map. Plus/minus keys change zoom. Controls and visible pins expose native accessibility actions.

Pins use the current HUD theme color. A solid center and steady ring remain visible while a thin ring expands and fades. Up to 12 visible markers pulse at once; the selected marker gets priority. Other markers stay static. Reduce Motion, disabled ambient animation and Low Power visual mode suppress these pulses. All map animations stop when its section is inactive or the HUD closes.

Up to 128 pins, their creation times, and the viewport are stored atomically in `~/Library/Application Support/EndfieldCharge/WorldMap/map.json`. Coordinates stay tied to the terrain while panning or zooming. The viewport saves when a gesture ends, rather than on every input event. Stored pins are never silently evicted when the limit is reached. Diagnostic `--ui-test` launches use a separate temporary store. Malformed archives and external edits are preserved rather than overwritten.

The bundled terrain is about 602 KB. Its 142,392 vertices and the bounded country geometry are decoded once on a utility queue into shared vector paths. A serial background worker clips and simplifies visible geometry, then draws relief, outlines, contours and hatching into one padded viewport image, capped at 1536 pixels per edge. Pointer input immediately transforms this image and the independently projected pins. Repainting is coalesced and cost-throttled during gestures, with a full-detail image after movement settles. Three wrap layers share a small world backdrop for coverage during rapid movement. There is no idle render loop or unbounded image history. No map downloads, runtime contour generation, polling or display-rate sampling run in the app. Pin pulses use Core Animation. The map does not access location services or track the user's location. This is generalized global terrain: zooming does not load street-level detail.
